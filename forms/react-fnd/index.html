Training resource. Confirm the patient-specific details and current program requirements before submitting.

Complete the referral

  1. Confirm that the patient meets the program criteria. In the existing EMU workflow, FND must be the clinically supported discharge diagnosis, rather than “spells.”
  2. Enter the patient’s name; review and correct every pre-entered answer.
  3. Sign the completed two-page referral.
  4. Include documentation confirming the FND diagnosis and the applicable diagnostic workup requested by the form.

EMU handoff

Follow the Weekend On Call handoff and faxing instructions when using this form in the EMU.
