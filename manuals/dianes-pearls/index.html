Rescue medication coverage

Diane’s report

Diane reported that Nayzilam was considered non-formulary and non-preferred and was not approved in the coverage situation described.

  1. Diazepam rectal gel: reported as preferred, with a supplied dose range of 2.5–20 mg as needed. This range records the source note; the individual dose must come from the patient’s prescribed rescue plan.
  2. If rectal diazepam is not tolerated: Diane reported Valtoco (diazepam nasal spray) as the preferred non-formulary alternative. The supplied note lists 5 mg or 10 mg as one spray in one nostril, and 15 mg or 20 mg total as one spray in each nostril using the two-device dose pack, as needed according to the prescribed rescue plan.

The single-device versus two-device distinction matches the Valtoco prescribing information. This pearl does not specify patient selection, repeat dosing, or a complete rescue plan.
