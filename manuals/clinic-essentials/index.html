Smartphrases & Templates

Find a SmartPhrase in Epic

  1. Go to My Tools → My SmartPhrases.
  2. Under USER, look up any attending, nurse practitioner, or second-year fellow.
  3. Find the phrase you need in that user’s saved phrases. For FND, look for the FND SmartPhrase.

How to Order ASM — The Easy Way

Use Epic’s epilepsy medication preference list to find orders with preset titration plans, rescue-medication doses, and refills.

  1. While placing an order, click the three dots and lines (list icon) next to the Search for new orders box. This opens the Preference List.
  2. In the left panel, select Medications, then Epilepsy.
  3. Select the medication order you need. Available entries include preset titration plans and rescue medications with prefilled doses and refills.
  4. Check the medication, dose, titration schedule, quantity, refills, and pharmacy for the patient before signing.
