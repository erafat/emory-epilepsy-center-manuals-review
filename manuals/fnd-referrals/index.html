Find the FND SmartPhrase in Epic

  1. In Epic, go to My Tools → My SmartPhrases.
  2. Under USER, look up the name of any attending, nurse practitioner, or second-year fellow.
  3. Look up the FND SmartPhrase in that user’s saved phrases.

Patient explanation

Adapt this wording only after the treating clinician has established the diagnosis.

Your events have been diagnosed as functional seizures, a form of functional neurological disorder (FND). You may also hear the terms dissociative seizures, nonepileptic events, or PNES. These symptoms are real and involuntary. We recommend treatment with a clinician experienced in FND; your care team may recommend cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) or another FND-focused treatment.

Finding treatment

  • Insurance: Call the behavioral health number on your insurance card to find an in-network therapist with experience treating FND.
  • ReACT FND: Telehealth treatment and contact form; 205-235-2010. See the ReACT referral form and checklist.
  • Psychology Today: Therapist directory; filter by insurance and therapy approach, then ask about FND experience.
  • Georgia Crisis & Access Line: 800-715-4225. Provides crisis support and help accessing behavioral health services. Georgia service information.

ReACT’s official website lists 205-235-2010, correcting the 202 area code in the supplied note. Use the official contact form for current inquiries.

Patient education

  • Nonepilepticseizures.com
  • Epilepsy Action: Functional (dissociative) seizures

The Emory epilepsy Psychiatry pathway has separate eligibility criteria: FND/PNES alone does not qualify for that pathway.
