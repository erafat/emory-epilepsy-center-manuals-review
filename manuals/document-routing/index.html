Clinic Essentials · Document Routing

What to Send to Whom

Route letters, forms, and orders to the appropriate team.

Rashunda / AdminFaith / Social WorkerRN / LPN

Rashunda / Admin

  • Letters — except medication and court letters; see Faith’s specific categories below.
  • Driving forms
  • MARTA Mobility forms
  • Parking permits
  • VA forms

Faith / Social Worker

  • Return to work / FMLA
  • Short-term disability (STD) and long-term disability (LTD) leave
  • Social Security Disability (SSDI) letters and forms
  • Citizenship forms
  • ALF / DMA 6
  • ADA reasonable accommodations
  • Diagnosis (DX) letters
  • Court letters

RN / LPN

  • Medication letters
  • OT / PT / SLP / home health orders
  • Seizure protocol forms
  • Medication authorization / medication assistance forms
  • Wheelchair evaluations

Letter categories

The supplied list assigns general letters to Admin, medication letters to nursing, and court letters to Faith. It also assigns diagnosis and SSDI letters to Faith. Confirm the recipient when a letter falls into more than one category.

Source: division routing list supplied September 25, 2026. “Accept Medication and Court” was interpreted as “except medication and court.” Staff assignments should be confirmed when they change.
