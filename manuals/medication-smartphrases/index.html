Contact the prescribing team immediately for a rash, and report dizziness, blurred vision, or balance problems. The label directs discontinuation at the first rash unless clearly unrelated to the drug; fever, blistering, mouth sores, or breathing difficulty require urgent medical assessment. Lamictal prescribing information.

Valproate raises lamotrigine exposure. Carbamazepine, phenytoin, phenobarbital, and primidone lower it. The supplied 100 mg/day endpoint is a selected template target, not a universal maintenance dose.
