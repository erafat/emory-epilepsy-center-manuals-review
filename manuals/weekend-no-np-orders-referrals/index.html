EMU Operations Resource

Weekend On Call

Provider checklist for Synergy orders and ReAct referrals when an NP is not available in the EMU.

Draft for colleague review — not approved institutional policy. Use only for patients who meet the relevant program criteria. Confirm diagnoses, referral answers, orders, and discharge documentation against the patient record and current local workflow.

Find the forms

Bulletin board

Synergy order forms and two-page ReAct referrals are on the bulletin board for weekends and other days when no NP is available in the EMU.

Provider Must Complete

Before handoff

Synergy orders

Complete all three items

  1. Put the patient’s name on the order.
  2. Complete the clinical orders.
  3. Sign the order.

ReAct referral — two pages

Confirm eligibility, answers, and signature

  1. Confirm that the clinician has diagnosed functional neurological disorder (FND) and documented it in the note, as required by the referral. If clinically accurate and supported, use FND rather than “spells” as the discharge diagnosis.
  2. Put the patient’s name on the referral.
  3. Review every pre-entered answer and correct anything that differs for this patient.
  4. Sign the completed two-page referral.

Handoff and Faxing

Next step

Usual workflow

Place completed forms on the NP desk

The NP team will fax the completed order or referral Monday morning.

NP away for a week

Fax to the designated EMU scheduling coordinator

If the NP is away for a week, the provider should fax the completed forms directly rather than leave them for the Monday handoff.

  1. Use the fax cover sheet and current fax number posted on the wall.
  2. Use the fax machine at the nurse’s station.
  3. Dial 9 before the external fax number.

The coordinator is arranging access to the EMU schedule so the patient can be looked up by name. The coordinator will add the non-clinical information and fax the order or referral with the required supporting material. Confirm the required attachments with the coordinator; the supplied message does not specify them completely.
