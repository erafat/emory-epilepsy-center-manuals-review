Fellow Must Complete

Synergy orders

Complete all three items

  1. Put the patient’s name on the order.
  2. Complete the clinical orders.
  3. Sign the order.

ReAct referral — two pages

Confirm eligibility, answers, and signature

  1. FND must be the discharge diagnosis for this ReACT referral—not “spells.” The diagnosis must be clinically supported and documented in the note.
  2. Put the patient’s name on the referral.
  3. Review every pre-entered answer and correct anything that differs for this patient.
  4. Sign the completed two-page referral.

Handoff and Faxing

Usual workflow

Place completed forms on the NP desk

The NP team will fax the completed order or referral Monday morning.

When no NP around (Weekend/Weekdays)

Fax to the designated EMU scheduling coordinator

When no NP is available, on weekends or weekdays, the fellow should fax the completed forms directly.

  1. Use the fax cover sheet and current fax number posted on the wall.
  2. Use the fax machine at the nurse’s station.
  3. On the hospital fax machine, dial 9 before the external fax number.

The coordinator is arranging access to the EMU schedule so the patient can be looked up by name. The coordinator will add the non-clinical information and fax the order or referral with the required supporting material. The online ReACT form requests documentation confirming the FND diagnosis and the applicable diagnostic workup. The Synergy form requests clinical records and a copy of the insurance card.

Doximity Fax

Use your activated Doximity fax account in accordance with local patient-information policy. Send to the same intended recipient specified in the handoff above.

Enter the recipient’s fax number directly. The leading 9 is for the hospital fax machine only.

From the website

  1. Open Doximity Fax, sign in, and select New.
  2. Select the recipient by fax number or saved contact. Add cover-page text as needed.
  3. Use the paperclip Add control to attach the completed, signed forms and required records. Review the recipient and all pages, then select Send.

From iPhone or iPad

  1. In Doximity, open Fax, unlock the inbox, and tap New Fax.
  2. Enter the recipient in To. Check Cc EHR: a previously entered number persists until removed.
  3. Tap + to attach a file or scan the signed forms. Document scanning requires iOS 18 or later. Include all required supporting records.
  4. Review the recipient, attachments, and cover page; tap the send arrow. Review the fax in the Sent tab and check its delivery status.

For image attachments, enable Contains Images (website: Attachments Contain Images) to preserve image quality. Keep attachments within Doximity’s 50 MB limit.
